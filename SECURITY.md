# Security Policy

Rolling Oaks Trucking Service takes the security of our website and the people who use it seriously.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability on rollingoaktrucking.com or in this repository, please report it privately rather than opening a public issue. You can report it using GitHub's private vulnerability reporting for this repository (Security tab, Report a vulnerability), or by emailing us directly at rollingoakservices@gmail.com with details of the issue and steps to reproduce it.

Please include as much information as you can, such as the affected page or endpoint, the type of issue, and any steps needed to reproduce it.

## What to Expect

We'll acknowledge your report as soon as we're able and let you know if we need more information. Since this is a small business site, response times may vary, but we take every report seriously and will follow up once the issue has been looked into.

Please do not publicly disclose a vulnerability until we've had a chance to address it.

## Scope

This policy covers the rollingoaktrucking.com website and its source code in this repository. For anything unrelated to security, such as general questions, support, or business inquiries, please use our regular contact info: (205) 420-3444 or rollingoakservices@gmail.com.
